# Month 5 Playable Slice Targets

Month 5 follows Month 4's basic Vic 2 substrate.

Theme: "Make the country loop playable through Unity."

Month 5 should not invent new deep simulation systems before the Month 4 POP/economy substrate is visible and useful. It should turn that substrate into a playable country-management loop.

## Scope

Include:

- Unity country dashboard polish
- province management UI
- building/construction UI polish
- budget controls
- market and POP feedback views
- event feed
- basic army stacks
- army movement
- battle v1
- war/peace v1
- simple diplomacy
- first 30-minute playable country test

Defer:

- civic trust
- advanced law rollout
- deep diplomacy
- LLM diplomacy
- newspapers
- disease
- climate
- sophisticated military logistics

## Dependency on Month 4

Month 5 assumes these Month 4 mechanics exist:

- POPs in provinces
- POP needs and fulfillment
- employment and unemployment
- RGOs, factories, artisans
- national market
- taxes and budget spending
- literacy, militancy, consciousness
- crude reform pressure
- Unity/admin inspection

Without those, Month 5 becomes UI over an empty economy. The playable slice should sit on real POP and market mechanics.

## Desired Demo

By the end of Month 5:

1. Start server.
2. Open Unity.
3. Select a country.
4. Inspect economy, POPs, and budget.
5. Change taxes/spending.
6. Build or expand production.
7. Watch employment, prices, and POP conditions respond.
8. Move an army.
9. Start and resolve a simple war.
10. Play for 30 minutes without needing admin-only tools.
