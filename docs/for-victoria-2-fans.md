# For Victoria 2 Fans

Victoria-Like is inspired by the design space that makes Victoria 2 unusually compelling: society, economy, politics, diplomacy, and war are coupled systems.

This project is not a remake. It does not use Paradox assets, event text, map data, or branding. It is a new open-source simulation that explores similar political-economy questions.

## What This Project Cares About

- POPs should be the heart of society.
- Politics should emerge from material conditions.
- Industry should matter because goods, wages, jobs, markets, and budgets are connected.
- War should be expensive social pressure, not a separate minigame.
- Expansion should eventually be about access, rank, markets, security, and influence, not only map painting.
- The simulation should be inspectable enough that players can understand why something happened.

## What Exists Now

The current code already simulates POPs, needs, production, markets, prices, taxes, spending, employment, unemployment, political pressure, and persistence. Unity can inspect a slice of that world.

The early demo is small on purpose. The goal is to show real systems responding to each other:

- Higher tax pressure changes cash and needs fulfillment.
- Production feeds market supply.
- Shortages and demand affect prices.
- Employment and unemployment affect POP conditions.
- Budget and military/state spending affect the country over time.

## What Is Not Promised Yet

The project does not yet have a full historical campaign, world market, finished diplomacy, deep reforms, spheres, polished war, or production-quality UI.

Those features are interesting only after the basic POP/economy loop is legible and trustworthy.

## How Fans Can Help

You can contribute without writing engine code:

- Add goods, factories, or balance notes.
- Create tiny scenarios.
- Write historical research notes.
- Improve docs that explain POP or market behavior.
- Test the demo and report confusing parts.
- Compare mechanics against Victoria 2 in design notes.
